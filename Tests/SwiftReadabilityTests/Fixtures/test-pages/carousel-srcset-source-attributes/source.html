Gallery sources from lazy images and pictures

The exhibition follows a river through the city, with photographs showing the same places in different seasons. Visitors can compare the changing light, the bridges, and the paths beside the water. Each photograph includes a caption describing its location. The article explains how the photographers returned throughout the year and chose views that make those changes easy to see.

The gallery belongs with this account of the exhibition. Its images and captions provide the examples discussed in the text, and their original addresses must survive extraction.
