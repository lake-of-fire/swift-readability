The research team followed the river through several villages and recorded how residents prepare for the changing seasons. Their detailed observations describe the work of local farmers, the history of the surrounding landscape, and the practical decisions that families make each year. These interviews form the central evidence of the article and explain why the project matters to the wider community. The report presents observation number 1 in the same careful detail.
