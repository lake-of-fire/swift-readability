The path followed the river through a stretch of old forest. We stopped at a wooden bridge to watch the water pass between the rocks, then continued along the eastern bank. The map showed a village beyond the next bend, where the walking route joined a quiet road.

At the village, a small notice described the history of the bridge and the people who had maintained it. Several families had used this crossing for generations. The riverside trees provided shade, and a narrow track connected the houses to the fields on the opposite bank.

By the afternoon, the clouds had cleared and the water reflected the hills. We returned by the same path, noting the junctions and landmarks for another visit. The journey was short enough for an easy day outside, with time to stop and look closely at the changing landscape.
